# nabnab 0.3.0

## What’s new

- **Research and audiobooks.** Browse arXiv versions, choose files from Zenodo records, and download selected LibriVox chapters into the audiobook shelf.
- **Personal servers.** Browse authorized Nextcloud and WebDAV endpoints, SFTP servers, and mounted network folders. Index remote references or copy files locally. Supported WebDAV and mounted-folder copies can resume after a verified version check.
- **Courses.** Organize lessons and saved materials for Rocketseat, Domestika, Coursera and Skillshare. Open official lesson links in your browser session, fetch Coursera Business outlines with authorized credentials, and queue supported Skillshare attachments. Available operations vary by provider.
- **Audio and video.** Switch between the full player and the floating mini player, use Quick Skip from the desktop, and adjust volume with the arrow keys. Browse music, imported playlists and audiobook chapters. Listen to local candidates before confirming a playlist match.
- **Personal archives.** Import selected categories from official Instagram, Facebook, X, Spotify and LinkedIn exports. The import review shows what is present and lets you choose a date range.
- **Local tools.** Upscale images, compare cutouts with their originals, export Telegram or WhatsApp stickers, separate vocals and instrumentals, and translate subtitles offline. Create a synchronized WAV or a dubbed MKV with an installed Windows voice. Optional models can be installed from Settings.
- **Device sharing.** Review a file, collection, link or text selection before handing it to KDE Connect. Check transfer progress and receipt in KDE Connect.
- **Interface and storage.** Home blends thirteen character patterns. The translucent sidebar expands on hover or focus. Voice cache limits and restart cleanup reduce leftover temporary files.

## Download

- `nabnab_0.3.0_x64-setup.exe` installs nabnab per user on Windows x64. The installer is unsigned and requires WebView2.
- `nabnab-browser-extension_0.3.0.zip` contains the Chrome and Edge extension. Extract it and follow the [setup and pairing guide](https://github.com/Hakaelll/nabnab/blob/v0.3.0/docs/BROWSER.md).
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the hashes for both downloads.

Provider access depends on the account and permissions. Some course workflows use the provider’s browser session and files saved through its own download controls. Live verification with course accounts and receipt on a physical KDE Connect device are pending. See the [project guide](https://github.com/Hakaelll/nabnab/tree/v0.3.0#readme) for requirements and tested workflows.
